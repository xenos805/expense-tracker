How calculated
Each active bill is normalized to a monthly amount (weekly × 52/12, biweekly × 26/12, monthly × 1, yearly ÷ 12). Temporary / installment bills count while today is on or before their end date (final payment). After the end date they are paid off and drop out of totals. Remaining-payments is an optional helper only.

Next due dates roll forward by cadence when a date passes (or when you mark paid). Biweekly total = monthly × 12 ÷ 26 · Yearly = monthly × 12.
